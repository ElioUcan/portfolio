## Overall Impression

Technically disciplined, aesthetically coherent. The critical miss is strategic: the page opens with "PORTFOLIO" in 9rem type instead of the person's name or a thesis statement. Fix H1, add section nav anchors, fix muted contrast, add 50 words of narrative.

## What's Working

1. Project descriptions are metric-specific and concrete (engineering-audience claims, not marketing adjectives).
2. List-based project layout respects how engineers read tabular information and avoids the card-grid trap.
3. Typographic and palette restraint keeps the aesthetic in "engineer" territory rather than drifting to "agency."

## Priority Issues

**[P0] "PORTFOLIO" is the h1 — announces category, not person**
Fix: Demote "PORTFOLIO" to a label; make name/role the dominant display element.

Fix: Add 4 anchor links to nav (#projects, #skills, #certs, #contact).

**[P2] Zero prose narrative — person is invisible**

**[P2] Three equal-weight CTAs create decision paralysis**
